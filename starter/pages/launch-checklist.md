Everything to check on launch day, {{date:+18}}.

## The day before

- [ ] Freeze changes on staging
- [ ] Back up the old site
- [ ] Tell the team the plan for the morning

## Launch morning

- [ ] Point the domain at the new site
- [ ] Place a real order, then refund it
- [ ] Check the site on a phone, a tablet and a laptop
- [ ] Send the newsletter

## After

- [ ] Watch for errors for a week
- [ ] Thank everyone 🎉
