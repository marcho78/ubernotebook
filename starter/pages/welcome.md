Omanote keeps your notes, plans, people and calendar in one place, on your computer, as plain files in {{folder}}. Nothing goes to a server.

These pages are examples of what it can do. Open them, change them, break them. When you're done, put them in the trash (each page's **⋯** menu).

## Two ways to write

These pages are **Pages**: a tree of pages made of blocks, the way Notion works. Omanote has a second space too, **Notebooks**: paper notebooks on a shelf, with covers, paper and pens, for writing that feels like writing. Switch between them at the top of the sidebar.
