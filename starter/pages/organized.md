Uber Notebook has a place for everything, and finds anything in a moment.

| To | Use |
|---|---|
| Find a page | **Ctrl+P**, then a few words. `#` and a name finds a tag |
| Make a page | **Ctrl+N**, or **+** beside a page in the sidebar for one inside it |
| Keep a page at hand | The ☆ at the top right: it's under *Favorites* in the sidebar |
| Group pages | Drag a page onto another in the sidebar, or type `/page` on a page |
| Track a project | *Make it a project* in a page's **⋯** menu: a status, a due date and how far along it is |
| Tag anything | **#** and a name in a line: #idea, #errand, #work/acme |
| Put a page away | *Archive* in its **⋯** menu. It stays searchable |
| Go back in time | *Page history* in a page's **⋯** menu: every version Uber Notebook kept |

## In the sidebar

- **Today**: what's left of today, from the calendar and your pages
- **Calendar** (**Ctrl+Shift+C**): your events, by month, week, agenda or compact
- **Library**: every link, file, PDF, picture, email and person you've put on a page, in one place
- **People**: your contacts. Name someone on a page with **@**
- **Projects**: every project, with how far along it is. See [[Website relaunch]]
- **Tags**: click one for every block that has it, page by page

## Questions

<details>
<summary>Where are my notes?</summary>

In {{folder}}: each page is a file of its own, named by its id. Settings can keep a Markdown copy of every page too, for Obsidian, git or any editor.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I bring my notes from Notion or Obsidian?</summary>

Yes: *Import…* at the foot of the sidebar takes files, or a whole folder (a Notion or Obsidian export, zipped or not). Pages inside pages, links, tables and pictures come along.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What if I delete something by mistake?</summary>

**Ctrl+Z** takes back almost anything. A page in the trash goes back where it was, and *Page history* has the versions of each page from before.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I lock a page so it doesn't change?</summary>

*Lock the page* in its **⋯** menu: it reads and copies, but nothing changes it until you unlock it.

</details>
