Lemon, garlic and chili spaghetti. Twenty minutes, one pot of water, serves two. #recipe

::columns 40 60
### Ingredients

- [ ] 200 g spaghetti
- [ ] 3 cloves of garlic, sliced thin
- [ ] 1 lemon, zest and juice
- [ ] A pinch of chili flakes
- [ ] 4 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] A handful of parsley
- [ ] Parmesan, to serve
::next
### Method

1. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water, a minute less than the packet says.
2. Warm the oil in a wide pan over low heat. Add the garlic and the chili, and cook until the garlic is pale gold, not brown.
3. Lift the pasta into the pan with a ladle of its water. Toss for a minute, until it's glossy.
4. Off the heat, add the lemon zest, the juice and the parsley. Season, and toss again.
5. Serve with Parmesan on top.
::end

> [!TIP] Keep a cup of the pasta water: a splash loosens the sauce if it gets too thick.

## Swaps

| Instead of | Try |
|---|---|
| Parsley | Basil, or rocket at the end |
| Chili flakes | A fresh chili, chopped |
| Parmesan | Toasted breadcrumbs |
